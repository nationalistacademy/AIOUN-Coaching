## 1. Executive summary

AIOU Coaching is a professional coaching, learning-support and documentation-guidance project operated by Nationalist Academy. Its first commercial priority is to provide clearly scoped professional coaching and consultancy-style support, with education and AIOU student support as the strongest subject-matter specialization.

The business should earn trust through useful guidance, transparent service boundaries, accurate sources, professional communication, privacy-conscious handling of documents and reliable delivery—not through exaggerated claims, guaranteed outcomes or misleading institutional branding.

## 2. Identity and positioning

### Public identity wording

“ AIOU Coaching is an independent coaching and educational guidance project operated by Nationalist Academy, a sole proprietorship operated by Faiez Mehmood in Karachi, Pakistan. It is not affiliated with Allama Iqbal Open University or any government authority.”

Use this wording consistently on About, Contact, Terms, service pages and relevant footer disclosures. Keep Nationalist Academy as the operator identity and AIOU Coaching as the public project brand.

### Positioning statement

Practical, accessible and responsible coaching for students, researchers, job seekers and individuals who need help understanding processes, improving skills and preparing professional documents.

### Audiences

- AIOU and other distance-learning students seeking study guidance.
- Students and early-career researchers learning research methods and academic writing.
- Job seekers preparing CVs, applications and interviews.
- Individuals seeking help understanding public procedures and organizing non-regulated documentation.
- Freelancers and small operators developing digital and professional skills.

Do not target or collect sensitive personal information unless it is essential to a clearly explained service.

## 3. Service pillars

1. Professional coaching and skills development.
2. Education and AIOU study support.
3. Research-methods and thesis-writing skills guidance.
4. CV, application and professional-document preparation.
5. General legal-information literacy and non-representational document organization.
6. Digital skills, freelancing and small-business learning.

The detailed service boundaries and examples are in [service-catalog.md](service-catalog.md) and [legal-boundaries.md](legal-boundaries.md).

## 4. Revenue model

Use transparent, service-based pricing. Each paid engagement must have a written quotation before work begins. The quotation should state:

- Service name and deliverables.
- What the client must provide.
- Included coaching time or document scope.
- Delivery date or estimated turnaround.
- Number of included revision rounds.
- Total fee, payment stages and any third-party costs.
- Cancellation, refund and rescheduling terms.
- What is explicitly excluded.

Do not publish invented “market rates” or imply a fixed fee until the proprietor has approved a real price list. A free introductory enquiry may be offered, but it must not be represented as a free professional consultation if substantive work is involved.

## 5. Client journey

1. Visitor reads the relevant service page and scope.
2. Visitor sends an enquiry through the official contact channel.
3. Operator checks fit, conflicts, urgency, competence and whether the request is regulated or inappropriate.
4. Client receives written scope, price, timeline, privacy notice and terms.
5. Work begins only after explicit acceptance and any agreed initial payment.
6. Operator provides coaching or draft deliverables within the agreed scope.
7. Client reviews the work and uses the included revision process.
8. Final delivery and closure confirmation are recorded.
9. Feedback is requested only with consent; testimonials are never fabricated.

See [client-workflow.md](client-workflow.md).

## 6. Trust and quality system

- Identify the operator and independent status accurately.
- Link to official AIOU, government and primary legal sources where relevant.
- Show publication and review dates on informational content.
- Distinguish facts, interpretation, examples and opinion.
- Disclose AI assistance where material to the service or where a client needs to know.
- Have a human review all public content and client deliverables.
- Keep a correction channel and respond to substantiated errors.
- Do not create fake reviews, fake credentials, fake office addresses or fake partnerships.

## 7. Marketing and growth

Prioritize educational articles, practical explainers, free tools, clear service pages, organic search and consent-based referrals. Content should answer genuine questions and help visitors decide whether the service is suitable.

SEO and generative-search optimization are quality and discoverability practices, not ranking guarantees. Do not use keyword stuffing, doorway pages, spun articles, fake schema, fabricated citations, misleading “official” wording or mass-generated low-value posts.

The Blogger site remains the public publishing surface. Repository content is a source of truth for approved drafts and documentation; repository changes do not automatically publish to Blogger.

## 8. Operating model

The proprietor is accountable for service acceptance, pricing, final quality, privacy, complaints and public claims. Automation may assist with research, drafting, formatting, link checks and reporting, but must not independently accept regulated work, make legal determinations, promise outcomes, publish posts or send client communications.

Maintain a simple record of enquiries and engagements with access controls and minimal personal data. Avoid storing identity documents or sensitive records unless essential and protected.

## 9. Risk register

| Risk | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Confusion with AIOU or government | Independent-status notice and careful naming |
| Unauthorized legal practice | Scope limits; refer regulated matters to qualified professionals |
| Academic misconduct | Coaching and learning support; client retains authorship |
| Incorrect dates or legal information | Primary-source verification and review dates |
| Privacy breach | Data minimization, access controls, retention and deletion process |
| Scope/payment disputes | Written quotation, acceptance and refund terms |
| AI hallucinations | Source checks and human approval |
| Unrealistic marketing claims | Evidence-based claims and no guarantees |

## 10. First 90-day sequence

### Foundation
- Approve service catalog and boundaries.
- Confirm contact channel, enquiry process and real pricing.
- Complete public-facing About, Contact, Terms, Privacy, Disclaimer and refund information.
- Establish a basic enquiry register with limited access.

### Controlled launch
- Publish a small number of high-quality service pages.
- Publish practical guides backed by official sources.
- Test enquiry acknowledgement and delivery workflow.
- Gather genuine feedback with permission.

### Review and improve
- Review enquiries, conversions, delivery time, complaints and refunds.
- Update service scope and pricing based on actual work.
- Review search performance without promising rankings.
- Expand only where competence, demand and capacity are demonstrated.

## 11. Decisions still requiring proprietor approval

- Exact service list to launch first.
- Actual fees, taxes and payment methods.
- Working hours and response-time promise.
- Refund and cancellation windows.
- Any in-person consultation address (do not invent or publish a home address).
- Whether any external qualified professionals will be engaged.
- Data retention duration and any third-party tools that process client data.

## 12. Success measures

Track qualified enquiries, accepted engagements, on-time delivery, client satisfaction, revision rates, refunds, substantiated complaints, repeat clients, useful-content engagement and organic search performance. Do not optimize solely for traffic or enquiry volume at the expense of suitability and quality.
