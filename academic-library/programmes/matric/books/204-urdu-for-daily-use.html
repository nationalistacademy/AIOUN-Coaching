Short study guidance

Is course ko active practice ke sath parhein. Rozmarra istemal ke alfaaz, reading comprehension aur clear written expression par focus karein. Mushkil vocabulary ke liye aik notebook rakhein: lafz, meaning aur apna banaya hua jumla likhein. Exact lesson topics official book se follow karein.

Simple daily practice

  1. Official book ka chhota passage read karein.
  2. New words underline karke unke meanings likhein.
  3. Passage ka central idea 3–4 sentences mein apne alfaaz mein likhein.
  4. Rozmarra situation par aik short paragraph ya dialogue practise karein.
  5. Apni spelling, sentence clarity aur punctuation check karein.

Assignment and exam preparation

Question ko dhyan se parhein aur jawab ko relevant points mein arrange karein. Saaf handwriting, correct spelling aur simple, complete sentences use karein. Book ke exercises revise karein aur apne weak vocabulary points dobara practise karein. Current instructions LMS/CMS se confirm karein.
