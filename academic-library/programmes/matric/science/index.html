Science study method

  • Physics: concept, known values, formula, substitution, units and verification.
  • Chemistry: symbols, terms, equations, observations and laboratory safety.
  • Biology: labelled diagrams, structure-function relationships and precise definitions.
  • Computer Science: understand the procedure, practise examples and record expected output.
  • Mathematics: show all steps and verify the result with substitution or estimation.
Chapter-wise books: The programme page confirms the course scheme, not each textbook contents page. Search each code in the official Soft Books portal and verify the exact edition before writing chapter summaries.
