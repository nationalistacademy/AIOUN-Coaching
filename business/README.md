## Operating principles

1. Be transparent about who operates the service and what is actually offered.
2. Sell coaching, education, research skills, document support and general information—not guaranteed academic or legal outcomes.
3. Never imply affiliation with Allama Iqbal Open University, a government department, a court, a bar council, or a regulator.
4. Do not claim to be an advocate, law firm, university, accredited institute, registered company, NGO or charity unless documentary proof and authorization support the exact claim.
5. Use written scope, price, timeline, revisions and cancellation terms before accepting paid work.
6. Minimize personal data, obtain consent, protect files and define retention periods.
7. Require human approval before public publishing, client delivery, or external communication.
8. Verify current official sources before stating university deadlines, legal rules, fees, eligibility or regulatory requirements.
