# Professional, Academic and Legal Boundaries

**Applies to:** AIOU Coaching, operated by Nationalist Academy  
**Jurisdiction context:** Public-facing operation in Karachi, Pakistan. This document is a risk-control policy, not a legal opinion.

## 1. Accurate business identity

Use the following verified wording:

> Nationalist Academy is a sole proprietorship operated by Faiez Mehmood in Karachi, Pakistan. AIOU Coaching is an education and professional-coaching project operated under Nationalist Academy.

The proprietor has stated that NTN status is registered. Do not publish the NTN number or imply any additional license, registration, incorporation, accreditation or tax clearance without evidence.

Do not describe Nationalist Academy as a company, corporation, NGO, charity, university, government body, law firm or regulated institution. Do not invent a street address, branch, office, phone number, professional membership or partner.

Official contact supplied for publication: faiezbinhafeez@gmail.com.

## 2. Independence from AIOU and public authorities

AIOU Coaching is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by Allama Iqbal Open University. AIOU names and marks belong to their respective owners. Use names only as needed to identify the educational context; do not copy branding in a way that implies official status.

The project is not a government portal and cannot issue official admission decisions, roll-number slips, degrees, transcripts, results, certificates or university approvals.

## 3. Legal information versus legal services

Permitted educational direction may include:
- General explanations of publicly available legal information.
- Links to official statutes, rules, government portals and court resources.
- General procedural literacy and document-organization checklists.
- Neutral administrative templates with clear instructions to verify applicability.

Do not provide or advertise:
- Case-specific legal advice or legal opinions.
- Representation before a court, tribunal, police, regulator or government office.
- Filing or signing documents as a lawyer or on a client's behalf.
- Litigation strategy, legal drafting for a live dispute, or interpretation requiring a qualified advocate.
- Claims of advocate, attorney, law-firm or legal-practitioner status without verified authorization.

Refer case-specific disputes, court matters, urgent deadlines and requests for representation to a qualified lawyer authorized in the relevant jurisdiction. Never imply that a disclaimer alone makes regulated legal work permissible.

## 4. Academic integrity

Support learning, not academic misconduct. Students retain responsibility for their own assessed work and submissions.

Do not offer:
- Exam impersonation or unauthorized live exam assistance.
- Plagiarism, contract cheating or ready-to-submit work falsely represented as the student's own.
- Fabricated data, citations, references, research findings, ethics approvals or attendance.
- Forged university documents, signatures or certificates.

Permitted support may include explaining concepts, teaching research methods, feedback, language editing within institutional rules, practice questions, formatting, study planning and self-assessment.

## 5. Claims and advertising

All public claims must be accurate, supportable and current. Do not promise:
- Guaranteed marks, admission, degree completion or exam success.
- Guaranteed jobs, earnings, clients, visas or business growth.
- Guaranteed Google rankings, indexing, featured snippets or AI citations.
- Guaranteed legal outcomes or government approvals.

Do not fabricate testimonials, reviews, customer counts, credentials, partnerships, success rates or case studies.

## 6. Privacy and confidentiality

- Collect only information necessary for the stated purpose.
- Explain why information is needed and how it will be used.
- Obtain consent before sharing material with external AI, cloud, analytics or document providers.
- Do not request passwords, OTPs, full card details or unnecessary identity documents.
- Restrict access to client files and use secure storage.
- Define retention, deletion, correction and complaint procedures in the public privacy policy.
- Obtain explicit permission before using client materials or feedback as examples or testimonials.

## 7. AI and automation

AI may assist with research discovery, drafting, formatting, translation and quality checks. A human must verify factual claims, citations, legal references, dates, calculations and client deliverables.

Do not submit confidential client content to third-party AI services without informed consent and a legitimate purpose. Do not let an agent independently publish, accept paid work, quote unapproved fees, send external messages or make professional determinations.

## 8. Sources and review

For university information, prefer current official AIOU notices and portals. For legal information, prefer primary legislation, official gazettes, court judgments and government sources. Record source title, URL and access/review date in research notes.

If an official source cannot be verified, label the information as unverified or omit it. Correct material errors promptly.

## 9. Referral and refusal

Decline requests that exceed competence, require unauthorized practice, involve fraud or misconduct, or cannot be delivered safely. Where appropriate, refer the person to the relevant official body or qualified professional without implying endorsement.

## 10. Review requirement

Before public launch, have the business terms, privacy wording, refund policy, tax statements, legal-information boundaries and any paid-service terms reviewed by an appropriately qualified Pakistan-based professional. This file is an internal operating safeguard and is not a substitute for legal review.
