AIOU Matric Course 217 — Food and Nutrition

Group: Matric General · Credits: 3 · Type: Elective

This guide turns the course into manageable learning areas and practical student activities. Read it alongside the current official AIOU course book.

Concept-wise learning areas

  1. major nutrients and their functions
  2. balanced diet and meal planning
  3. food hygiene and storage
  4. nutrition needs across life stages
  5. healthy food choices

Practical learning activity

Plan a balanced meal using locally available foods and identify its main nutrient sources.

How to make chapter notes

  1. Record the exact unit title and page numbers from the book.
  2. Write the main concept in simple Roman English.
  3. Explain key terms and processes in your own words.
  4. Add a real-life example, diagram, calculation or practical checklist where suitable.
  5. Attempt the official exercise questions and mark weak points.

Assignment and exam preparation

  • Answer the question that is asked; do not add unrelated material.
  • Use headings, examples and clear steps.
  • For practical courses, learn safety, sequence, tools and record keeping.
  • For language courses, practise reading, vocabulary and sentence building regularly.
  • Check current assessment instructions in your LMS/CMS.
