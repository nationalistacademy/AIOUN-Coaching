Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

AIOU Coaching provides independent educational resources, study guidance and free learning tools. This policy explains the information visitors may share and how it should be handled when using this website.

1. Independent educational platform

AIOU Coaching is an independent platform. It is not owned, operated, affiliated with, endorsed by or authorized by Allama Iqbal Open University (AIOU). It is not an official AIOU website, CMS help desk or representative. AIOU alone controls its admissions, CMS, student records, official fees and administrative decisions.

2. Information you choose to provide

If you contact us, you may choose to provide your name, email address and the details of your general educational question. Please share only the minimum information needed. Do not post personal or confidential information in public comments.

Never send us: CMS passwords, OTPs, recovery codes, security answers, session tokens, CNIC images, payment-card details or other authentication secrets. We do not need these details to provide general learning guidance.

3. CMS accounts and student records

Students must remain in control of their own AIOU accounts and confidential correspondence. AIOU Coaching will not log in to a student's CMS account, collect or use credentials, reset or retrieve an account, submit a CMS Student Service Request for a student, or email AIOU on a student's behalf about confidential account matters.

For an account-specific issue, submit your own request through the CMS Student Service Request facility, contact AIOU personally from your own email address, or visit the relevant AIOU Regional Office. A contact address mentioned in previous correspondence is not a guarantee of current availability; verify contact details on AIOU's official website before sending sensitive information.

4. Cookies, analytics and advertising

Blogger, Google services, embedded content, analytics tools or advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own settings and policies. The technologies actually active on this website may change. You can review your browser settings and the relevant provider policies to manage available choices. Do not assume that disabling cookies will leave every site feature unaffected.

5. How information is used

Information voluntarily submitted through a contact method may be used to understand and respond to your general question, maintain site safety and improve educational content. Do not send information that is not necessary for that purpose. We do not promise that an external service provider will process information in a particular way; review that provider's own privacy terms.

6. Third-party links and services

This website may link to AIOU or other third-party websites and services. Those websites operate independently and have their own terms, privacy notices and security practices. Check the destination carefully before sharing information or making a payment.

7. Official fees and optional services

Official AIOU fees are determined by AIOU and must be checked through official AIOU channels. Any optional third-party service charge, if offered, is separate from official university fees and must have its scope, total price, payment method and refund or cancellation terms disclosed before you agree. Payment does not guarantee admission, enrollment, a result change, account recovery or any university decision.

8. Academic integrity

Our educational resources and tools are intended to help learners understand topics, practise, plan and improve their own work. Students remain responsible for the authorship and submission of assessed work through their own authorized accounts. We do not provide impersonation, contract cheating, guaranteed marks or submission on a student's behalf.

9. Contact

For general website or learning-resource questions, email faiezhafeez@gmail.com. Do not include CMS credentials, OTPs, recovery codes or confidential student records in your message.

10. Policy updates

This policy may be updated when website features, applicable requirements or student-support procedures change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.

Important reminder: Never share your CMS password, OTP or recovery code with AIOU Coaching or any third party. Handle confidential AIOU account matters personally through official AIOU channels.
