Quick answer

AIOU Coaching is an independent website that helps Allama Iqbal Open University students understand admissions, assignments and exams. We are not AIOU and we do not issue degrees, results or official documents.

Who we are

AIOU Coaching is an independent online study-support resource. We publish plain-language guides and practical tools to help learners find and understand study information.

What we offer

  • Admission guidance for each programme and round
  • Assignment help and solved-assignment resources
  • Academic calendar and deadline reminders
  • Exam preparation tips and guess-paper style revision notes

Accuracy and corrections

We aim to make our guides clear and useful. University dates, fees, policies and procedures can change, so readers should confirm important requirements against the latest official AIOU notice. We do not claim that every page has been independently reviewed by a tutor. If you find an error, please report it through our contact page.

Independence notice

AIOU Coaching is not affiliated with Allama Iqbal Open University. Always confirm official requirements on the university's own website. See our Disclaimer for details.
