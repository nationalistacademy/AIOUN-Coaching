# AIOU Coaching — academic integrity, legal, privacy, and OSINT guardrails

This is an operational product policy, not legal advice. Obtain review from a qualified Pakistani lawyer before relying on it as a complete legal compliance programme.

## 1. Independent identity and truthful claims
- Describe AIOU Coaching as an independent educational support platform.
- Do not use AIOU logos, official seals, or wording that implies institutional ownership, partnership, authorization, or endorsement unless written permission exists.
- Add a clear disclaimer on relevant public pages: “AIOU Coaching is an independent educational support platform and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Allama Iqbal Open University.”
- Do not guarantee admission, degree completion, marks, exam success, search rankings, AdSense approval, or official processing outcomes.
- Identify the actual operator/business details accurately. Do not invent registration, licence, tax, or professional credentials.

## 2. Academic integrity by design
Permitted support:
- Explain assignment prompts, course concepts, and marking rubrics.
- Help students plan, outline, research, and understand sources.
- Generate practice questions and worked examples for learning.
- Give feedback on student-written drafts and explain corrections.
- Help with spelling, clarity, citations, formatting, and accessibility.
- Create blank templates, study notes, and self-assessment materials.

Disallowed support:
- Completing assessed work for submission as if authored by the student.
- Contract cheating, plagiarism, impersonation, or evading assessment controls.
- Fabricating sources, citations, fieldwork, survey results, data, or research findings.
- Forging transcripts, certificates, identity documents, tutor signatures, or official notices.
- Misrepresenting AI-generated text as verified original research.

Product controls:
- Label AI-generated suggestions as drafts or learning aids.
- Encourage students to verify facts and citations and rewrite in their own words.
- Make user review and explicit approval mandatory before export, sharing, or publication.
- Do not auto-submit assignments to AIOU or any institution.
- Keep source links and generation dates where relevant.

## 3. Privacy and security
- Publish a plain-language privacy notice before collecting personal information.
- Define purpose, legal basis, data categories, retention, deletion/contact route, and third-party processors.
- Minimize collection; do not request CNIC, passwords, OTPs, bank/card data, or unrelated sensitive information for ordinary study tools.
- Keep student uploads, account details, quiz attempts, marks, and enrollment information private and access-controlled.
- Use server-side authorization for protected data; never trust client-side role flags.
- Do not place API keys, service-account credentials, tokens, or private keys in browser code or Git.
- Do not send user writing to external AI providers without clear disclosure and consent.
- Provide a non-AI/manual alternative where feasible.
- Define an incident response process: contain, preserve logs/evidence, assess impact, notify affected parties/regulators where legally required, and document remediation.

## 4. Legal information and professional boundaries
- General legal explainers must identify jurisdiction and date, cite current primary/official sources, and distinguish law from opinion.
- Do not call the service a law firm or offer legal representation unless the operator is duly qualified and authorized and the service has been separately approved.
- Refer case-specific disputes, notices, court matters, tax advice, and regulatory filings to a qualified Pakistani lawyer or relevant licensed professional.
- Preserve original notices, emails, receipts, and timestamps; do not alter evidence.
- Never fabricate legal notices, threaten action without basis, or impersonate regulators, lawyers, institutions, or government officials.

## 5. OSINT and research ethics
- Use only lawful, publicly accessible sources or sources for which permission exists.
- Have a specific legitimate purpose and collect the minimum necessary information.
- Record source, access date, and confidence; distinguish verified fact from inference.
- Do not bypass authentication, access controls, paywalls, or technical restrictions.
- Do not doxx, stalk, harass, deanonymize private individuals, harvest credentials, or build sensitive personal profiles.
- Do not publish personal data merely because it is technically discoverable.
- For security testing, obtain written authorization and define scope before testing.

## 6. Copyright and educational material
- Prefer official/publicly licensed sources and original explanations.
- Respect copyright, licence terms, attribution, and takedown requests.
- Do not mirror entire textbooks, paid keybooks, or copyrighted course packs without permission.
- Use limited quotations only where lawful and necessary; link to the original source.
- Keep a record of permissions and licence notices for contributed assets.

## 7. Website, advertising, and consumer trust
- Make ownership/contact information, service scope, terms, privacy policy, refund/cancellation terms (if paid services ever exist), and disclaimers easy to find.
- Avoid deceptive buttons, forced redirects, fake testimonials, misleading download links, ad placements that mimic navigation, and ads that obstruct content.
- Separate editorial content from advertising and disclose sponsorship/affiliate relationships.
- Do not buy traffic, click own ads, encourage ad clicks, or promise AdSense acceptance.
- Keep substantive, original, student-helpful content; avoid thin, duplicated, auto-generated pages.

## 8. Legal verification checklist before launch
- [ ] Confirm operator's legal name and business form from owner-approved documents.
- [ ] Confirm which registrations, tax obligations, consumer rules, and licences apply to the actual services and revenue model.
- [ ] Have a Pakistani lawyer review Terms, Privacy Policy, disclaimers, user-generated-content rules, and any paid-service terms.
- [ ] Confirm lawful basis and retention for student data and third-party AI processing.
- [ ] Confirm copyright permissions and takedown contact.
- [ ] Confirm advertising disclosures and platform policy compliance.
- [ ] Keep a dated record of legal review and policy revisions.

Never present this checklist as proof that the project is legally compliant. Compliance depends on facts, current law, actual operations, and professional review.
