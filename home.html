FAQ

Common questions about AIOU coaching.

What is AIOU coaching?+

AIOU coaching is guided support for Allama Iqbal Open University students. It covers study planning, assignment guidance, workshop preparation and exam revision.

Which AIOU programs do you coach?+

We coach Matric, FA and F.Sc, BA and B.Sc, B.Ed, MA and M.Sc, M.Ed and MPhil. Message us your program and course codes to confirm availability.

Is AIOU coaching available online?+

Yes. Sessions and replies run online, so you can study from any city in Pakistan.

How much does it cost?+

Fees depend on your program and semester. Starting from PKR 999. We share the exact amount before you start, with no hidden charges.

Will you write my assignments for me?+

No. We explain the questions and help you structure answers so the work you submit is your own.

Talk to a tutor before you pay for anything.
