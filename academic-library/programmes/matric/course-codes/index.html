AIOU Matric General Course Codes

Current AIOU Matric General programme: 48 credit hours. Compulsory courses are offered in the first three semesters; electives are selected in semester four. Use your current admission scheme/CMS as final confirmation.

Elective combinations: AIOU lists any 2 courses of 6 credits, OR 1 course of 6 credits plus 2 courses of 3 credits, OR 4 courses of 3 credits. Course 208 and 210 have a prerequisite note.
