# Pricing, Quotes and Payment Policy — AIOU Coaching

**Status:** Draft policy. Actual rates and payment channels require proprietor approval before publication.

## 1. Transparent quotations

AIOU Coaching will not invent or imply approved prices. Each paid service must receive a written quote before the client commits.

A quote should include:

- Service and scope.
- Deliverables and format.
- Client responsibilities and required inputs.
- Fee and currency.
- Any applicable taxes or third-party costs, clearly identified and verified.
- Payment stages and due dates.
- Estimated turnaround.
- Included revisions and what counts as new work.
- Cancellation, rescheduling and refund terms.
- Quote expiry date, if applicable.

## 2. No hidden charges

Do not add fees outside the accepted quote without explaining the change and receiving the client's agreement. Third-party subscriptions, courier fees, printing, government fees and platform charges must be identified separately where relevant.

## 3. Payment methods

Only publish payment methods that the proprietor has verified, controls and can reconcile. Never request passwords, one-time passcodes, full card details or banking credentials by email or chat. Do not store payment-card data.

## 4. Deposits and milestones

If a deposit or milestone payment is used, state the amount or percentage, what work it covers, and what happens if either party cancels. Do not describe a deposit as non-refundable unless the term is lawful, fair, clearly disclosed and reviewed for the applicable jurisdiction.

## 5. Cancellation, rescheduling and refunds

Before launch, the proprietor must set clear and lawful rules for:
- Cancellation before work begins.
- Cancellation after work begins.
- Rescheduling coaching sessions.
- Missed appointments and late arrival.
- Work not delivered by the agreed date.
- Material failure to provide the agreed scope.
- Third-party charges that cannot be recovered.

Assess refund requests fairly and document the outcome. Do not promise “no refunds under any circumstances.”

## 6. Free enquiries

A free initial enquiry may be used to identify the client's needs and service fit. State its duration and limits. Detailed review, written advice, research or document preparation should be treated as paid work only after a written quote is accepted.

## 7. No outcome guarantees

Fees purchase the defined service and time, not a guaranteed mark, admission, job, income, search ranking, legal result, visa, approval or other third-party decision.

## 8. Publication gate

This policy is not ready for public use until the proprietor has approved actual fees, taxes, payment channels, deposit rules, cancellation windows, rescheduling rules and refund process. Obtain appropriate local professional review where needed.
