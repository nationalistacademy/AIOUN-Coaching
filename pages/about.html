Quick answer

AIOU Coaching is an independent website that helps Allama Iqbal Open University students understand admissions, assignments and exams. We are not AIOU and we do not issue degrees, results or official documents.

Who we are

We are a small team of tutors and former distance-learning students. We write plain-language guides so that students can find answers without searching through many pages.

What we offer

  • Admission guidance for each programme and round
  • Assignment help and solved-assignment resources
  • Academic calendar and deadline reminders
  • Exam preparation tips and guess-paper style revision notes

How we keep content accurate (E-E-A-T)

Experience
Our guides are written by people who have studied or tutored in distance-learning programmes.
Expertise
Each guide is reviewed by a tutor in the relevant subject before publishing.
Authoritativeness
We point students to AIOU's official notices for fees, dates and rules.
Trust
Every post shows its last-updated date. Tell us about mistakes through the contact page.

Independence notice

AIOU Coaching is not affiliated with Allama Iqbal Open University. Always confirm official requirements on the university's own website. See our Disclaimer for details.
