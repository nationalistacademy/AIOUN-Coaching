01. Introduction

AIOU Coaching ("hum", "hamara", "humein") ek independent Pakistani education platform hai jo AIOU aur tamam Pakistani boards ke students ke liye study material, solved assignments, guess papers, past papers aur coaching services provide karta hai.

Ye Privacy Policy aapko batati hai ke jab aap hamari website aioucoaching.blogspot.com visit karte hain, ya hamari services use karte hain, to hum aapki personal information kaise collect karte hain, kaise use karte hain, aur kaise protect karte hain.

Is website ko use karke aap is Privacy Policy ki shartein accept karte hain. Agar aap agree nahi karte, to please website use na karein.

02. Information We Collect

Hum ye types ki information collect karte hain:

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Contact details: Name, email address, WhatsApp number — jab aap contact form ya WhatsApp par message karte hain.
  • Order details: Program name, course codes, semester — jab aap material order karte hain.
  • Payment information: JazzCash/EasyPaisa/Bank transfer screenshots — payment confirmation ke liye.
  • Communication: WhatsApp conversations, emails — jo aap humse share karte hain.

2.2 Information Automatically Collected

  • Device information: Browser type, operating system, screen resolution.
  • Log data: IP address (anonymized), access time, pages visited, referral URL.
  • Usage data: Kaunse pages zyada dekhe, kitna time bitaya, kaunse links click kiye.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Google Analytics: Aggregated traffic data (anonymized).
  • Google Search Console: Search queries se aane wala traffic.
  • WhatsApp Business: Message metadata (delivery time, read status).

07. Data Retention

Hum aapki information sirf us waqt tak retain karte hain jab tak zaroori hai:

  • Order data: 2 saal (accounting + refund purposes).
  • WhatsApp messages: 1 saal (customer support history).
  • Payment screenshots: 90 din.
  • Analytics data: Google Analytics default (26 months).
  • Contact form submissions: 6 months.

Retention period khatam hone par data securely delete ya anonymize kar diya jata hai.

08. Data Security

Hum aapki information protect karne ke liye reasonable security measures use karte hain:

  • HTTPS encryption: Hamari website fully HTTPS enabled hai.
  • Access controls: Aapki information sirf authorized team members access kar sakte hain.
  • Secure communication: WhatsApp end-to-end encrypted communication use karta hai.
  • Regular backups: Data loss prevent karne ke liye regular backups.
  • Account security: Two-factor authentication (2FA) sensitive accounts par enabled.

Lekin, koi bhi internet-based transmission 100% secure nahi hoti. Hum best effort karte hain lekin absolute security guarantee nahi kar sakte.
