Quick answer

If you own copyright in something published here without permission, send us a notice with the details below. We review valid notices and remove infringing material promptly.

What to include in a notice

  1. Your name, address, email and phone number.
  2. A description of the copyrighted work you say was infringed.
  3. The exact URL of the page on this site that contains it.
  4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorised.
  5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that your notice is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner.
  6. Your physical or electronic signature.

Where to send it

Use the contact page and write "DMCA Notice" in the message, or email [YOUR-DMCA-EMAIL].

Counter-notice

If you believe material was removed by mistake, send a counter-notice with the removed URL, a statement that you believe the removal was an error, and your contact details.

Repeat infringers

We may block users who repeatedly post infringing material.
