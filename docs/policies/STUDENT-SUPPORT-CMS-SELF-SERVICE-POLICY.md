# Student Support, CMS Privacy & Self-Service Policy

**Status:** Repository policy draft for review. This document does not itself change or publish any live Blogger page.
**Applies to:** AIOU Coaching (independent student-learning tools and guidance).
**Contact:** faiezhafeez@gmail.com

## 1. Platform role
AIOU Coaching provides independent learning tools, study guidance and educational resources. It is not operated by AIOU and is not an AIOU regional office, official LMS/CMS support desk, agent or authorized representative. AIOU alone controls its CMS, admissions, student records, official charges and administrative decisions.

## 2. CMS account boundary
Students must personally control their CMS accounts and confidential correspondence. AIOU Coaching must never request, collect, store, view, use or transmit CMS passwords, OTPs, recovery codes, security answers, session tokens or other authentication secrets.

The platform and its contributors must not:
- log in to a student's CMS account or operate it for them;
- request, retrieve, reset or forward student credentials;
- submit a CMS Student Service Request for a student;
- email AIOU on a student's behalf about credentials, admission, enrollment, results, tutor details or other confidential student records; or
- represent themselves as the student or as an AIOU-authorized intermediary.

For account-specific help, explain the general self-service route and ask the student to submit their own request through the CMS **Student Service Request** facility, contact AIOU personally from their own email address, or visit the relevant AIOU Regional Office. The address **ad.adms@aiou.edu.pk** was supplied in AIOU correspondence; students should verify current contact information on AIOU's official website before sending sensitive information.

## 3. Learning support versus administrative intervention
AIOU Coaching may explain public instructions, help students understand general processes, provide original study material, practice questions and free learning tools. It cannot access student records, verify an individual's account, change university data, make admissions decisions, guarantee a university response or intervene in confidential cases.

## 4. Privacy and minimum data
Do not ask students to post CNICs, phone numbers, addresses, roll numbers, CMS screenshots or account identifiers in public comments. Never ask for passwords, OTPs or recovery codes under any circumstances. If sensitive information is shared accidentally, do not repeat or publish it; direct the student to AIOU's official channel and advise them to secure their account.

## 5. Fees and disclosure
Clearly distinguish official AIOU fees from any optional third-party service charge. AIOU Coaching must not label a private charge as an official university fee or imply that payment guarantees admission, enrollment, a result correction, credential recovery or any other university action. Any optional paid service must disclose its full scope, total price, payment method and refund/cancellation terms before the student agrees. Free tools must be labelled accurately as free; no hidden fee or credential-sharing requirement may be attached.

## 6. Academic integrity
Tools should support students in understanding questions, planning, drafting, checking and improving their own work. Students must remain the authors of assessed submissions and must submit work through their own authorized university accounts. Do not offer impersonation, contract cheating, guaranteed marks or assignment submission on a student's behalf.

## 7. Student-support workflow
1. Classify the request as general learning support or confidential/account-specific administration.
2. For learning support, use public official sources and original explanations; identify outdated or unverified information.
3. For CMS or personal-record matters, do not collect credentials or send emails/requests for the student. Direct the student to self-service through CMS, their own email, or the Regional Office.
4. Explain that only AIOU can confirm official fees, account status, deadlines and administrative outcomes.
5. Collect the minimum information necessary for non-sensitive educational help and do not retain unnecessary personal data.

## 8. Public-facing notice
Suggested short notice for the website:

> AIOU Coaching is an independent educational-support platform and is not affiliated with or authorized by Allama Iqbal Open University (AIOU). We provide learning resources and general guidance, not access to university accounts or student records. Never share your CMS password, OTP or recovery code with us or anyone else. You must submit your own CMS Student Service Requests and contact AIOU personally for confidential account matters. Official AIOU fees are separate from any optional third-party service charges, which must be disclosed in advance.

## 9. Review
Review this policy when AIOU updates its official student-support channels or when the platform's services change. This is an operational policy, not legal advice.
