Research degrees: knowledge ko investigate karna

MS, MPhil aur PhD level par sirf textbook summary kaafi nahi hoti. Student ko research problem define karna, literature critically review karna, appropriate method choose karna, evidence analyse karna aur findings clearly report karna hota hai. Programme-specific rules AIOU prospectus aur department se verify karein.

Research journey

  1. Research question: Clear, focused, answerable aur ethically appropriate.
  2. Literature review: Existing research ko compare karein; sirf source-by-source summary na likhein.
  3. Methodology: Design, sample, tools, procedure aur analysis ka reason explain karein.
  4. Analysis: Data ko research question ke mutabiq interpret karein; limitations mention karein.
  5. Writing: Citations, tables, figures aur references consistent rakhein.
Academic integrity: fabricated data, copied thesis text, fake citations aur uncredited AI-generated claims use na karein. AI ko explanation/planning aid samjhein, evidence ka replacement nahi.
