# Client enquiry workflow — draft operating procedure

**Owner:** Nationalist Academy  
**Project:** AIOU Coaching  
**Mode:** Human review and approval required  
**Automation:** No auto-reply, no external message sending, no payment capture and no automatic acceptance.

## 1. Enquiry channels

Interim public draft uses the confirmed business email only: `faiezbinhafeez@gmail.com`.

The legacy Formspree endpoint and WhatsApp link must not be treated as verified until the proprietor confirms ownership, access, monitoring and data handling. See `business/privacy-data-inventory.md`.

## 2. Intake and minimum data

For the first message, request only a short description, service category and reply email. Name is optional unless needed. Do not ask for passwords, OTPs, identity documents, bank/card details, student portal credentials, full confidential case files or unrelated sensitive information.

## 3. Human triage

The proprietor reviews each enquiry and classifies it:

- **In scope:** proceed to a written proposal.
- **Needs clarification:** ask for only the minimum missing information.
- **Out of scope:** politely decline or suggest an appropriate public/official resource.
- **Regulated / high risk:** do not provide case-specific legal advice or representation; refer the person to an appropriately qualified and authorized professional.
- **Academic-integrity concern:** decline plagiarism, contract cheating, impersonation, fabricated data/references or assessed-work substitution; offer learning-focused guidance instead.

## 4. Written proposal

Before accepting paid work, provide:
- Exact service and scope
- Deliverables and exclusions
- Fee and payment method (only after proprietor approval)
- Start condition and timeline
- Revision/feedback limits
- Cancellation and refund terms
- Client responsibilities and required inputs

No proposal is automatically accepted. No work begins before the client accepts the written scope and agreed payment conditions.

## 5. Delivery and closure

- Human review every deliverable before sending.
- Keep the client responsible for their own academic submission and professional decisions.
- Do not promise marks, admission, job placement, legal outcome, regulator approval or third-party decision.
- Close the engagement with a short completion note and handle records under an approved retention schedule.

## 6. Communication and publishing controls

- AI may help draft, organize, research and quality-check.
- AI must not autonomously accept a client, quote a fee, send an email/message, publish a public page, make a regulated-service decision or issue a refund.
- The proprietor approves all outward-facing communication and public content.
- Do not store credentials, payment-card information or unnecessary identity documents.
