Student questions

Frequently asked

Is AIOU Coaching officially affiliated with AIOU?

No. AIOU Coaching is an independent student-support platform and is not an official website of Allama Iqbal Open University.

Can I use the tools on mobile?

Yes. The homepage and student tools are designed to work across phones, tablets and desktop browsers.

Where can I find the complete toolkit?

Use the Student Toolkit button above or open the AIOU Tool Kit page from the site navigation.

Can AI-generated work be submitted directly?

AI output should be checked, personalised and used responsibly. Follow your course instructions and academic-integrity requirements before submitting any work.

Important: AIOU Coaching is an independent educational support platform. Always verify official dates, admissions, results, assignments and examination information through the official AIOU channels.
