Associate Degree: programme aur electives samjhein

Associate Degree undergraduate level ka programme hai. AIOU ke official BA/Associate Degree page par 2-year duration, 66 credit hours aur ODL mode show hota hai for the cited Associate Degree in Arts page. Har Associate Degree ka structure ek jaisa assume na karein; Arts, Science, Social Sciences, Commerce aur face-to-face options ka current prospectus alag check karein.

Credit hours aur electives

Credit hours course workload ko show karte hain. Compulsory courses sab relevant students ke liye required hote hain; elective courses mein approved list aur programme rules ke mutabiq choice hoti hai. Apne semester ki scheme ke baghair elective select na karein.

Subject ko master karne ka tareeqa

  1. Course code aur title dono match karein.
  2. Book ke unit-wise learning outcomes likhein.
  3. Definitions ko apni language mein samjhein.
  4. Numerical subjects mein solved example ke baad similar question khud solve karein.
  5. Assignment answer mein reasoning, steps aur references include karein.
