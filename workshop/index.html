Workshop Planner

Enter the workshop details you have received from AIOU and create a calendar reminder. The reminder file is generated on your device; this page does not fetch private student records or send your information to a server.

Reminder: Confirm date, time, course and venue from the official AIOU CMS / workshop notice before saving. Import the downloaded .ics file into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook. Automatic Google Calendar account access is not enabled on this static tool.
