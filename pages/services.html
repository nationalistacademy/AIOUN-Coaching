Our service areas

1. Professional coaching

Structured one-to-one or small-group guidance focused on practical goals and skill development.

  • Goal clarification and learning plans
  • Digital and workplace skills coaching
  • Freelancing and professional-development guidance
  • Progress review and action points

2. AIOU & distance-learning support

Independent guidance to help students understand study processes and prepare more effectively.

  • Programme and course information orientation
  • Assignment planning and concept explanation
  • Exam preparation and practice-question guidance
  • Study schedules and learning resources

3. Research skills & thesis guidance

Learning support that helps students develop their own research capability and academic voice.

  • Topic exploration and research-question refinement
  • Research proposal structure guidance
  • Literature-search and source-evaluation skills
  • Methodology concepts and writing feedback

4. CV & professional documents

Practical assistance with organizing information and presenting a person's own experience clearly.

  • CV structure and proofreading
  • Job application and cover-letter guidance
  • Interview preparation
  • Formatting and document quality checks

5. Digital tools & productivity

Guidance on using accessible digital tools responsibly for study and work.

  • Document and spreadsheet skills
  • Online research and source management
  • Responsible AI and digital-workflow literacy
  • Basic website/content workflow guidance

6. General legal-information literacy

General public information and help organizing questions or documents for discussion with an appropriately qualified professional.

  • Plain-language explanations of publicly available information
  • Finding official laws, forms and government sources
  • Non-representational document organization
  • Preparing questions for a qualified lawyer or authority

Not included: legal advice, legal opinions, representation, court appearances, drafting or filing as a lawyer, or acting as an advocate.
