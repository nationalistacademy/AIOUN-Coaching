AIOU Matric Dars-e-Nizami — Course Codes

AIOU lists Dars-e-Nizami as a separate secondary certificate route for eligible Deeni Madaris/Jamia students. The official page describes a two-year, four-semester ODL programme with 48 credit hours.

SemesterCourse codes and titles
1202 Pakistan Studies; 240 Quran-e-Hakeem
2204 Urdu; 207 Compulsory English-I; 247 Mathematics-I
3203 General Science; 221 Compulsory English-II; 248 Mathematics-II
4241 Islamic Fiqh; 242 Seerat-e-Tayyeba; 246 Arabic

Subject study method

  • Quran-e-Hakeem: note key vocabulary, central message and references from the assigned book.
  • Islamic Fiqh: distinguish terms, principles and the book's organisation of rulings; use verified sources.
  • Seerat-e-Tayyeba: prepare timelines and connect events with lessons in character and community life.
  • Arabic: practise vocabulary, reading and grammar in short daily sessions.
  • Shared subjects: use another track's guide only after confirming the course code and book edition are the same.
Chapter accuracy: The official programme page confirms courses and semester order, but not the current books' contents pages. Exact chapter explanations require the matching PDF edition.
