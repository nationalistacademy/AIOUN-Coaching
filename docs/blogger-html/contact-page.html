CMS and student-account matters

AIOU Coaching cannot access, operate, recover or change a student's CMS account. We do not collect CMS passwords, OTPs, recovery codes or session tokens. We also cannot submit a CMS Student Service Request or send confidential emails to AIOU on a student's behalf.

If your issue concerns your admission, enrollment, tutor, assignment record, result, account access or another personal student record, please:

  1. Sign in to your own AIOU CMS account and submit the request yourself through the Student Service Request facility, where applicable.
  2. Alternatively, contact AIOU personally from your own email address or visit the relevant AIOU Regional Office.
  3. Verify the latest official contact details and instructions on the AIOU website before sending personal information.

A contact address previously mentioned in correspondence is ad.adms@aiou.edu.pk; please verify that it is current and appropriate on AIOU's official website before use. AIOU alone can confirm account status, official fees, deadlines and administrative decisions.
