What we focus on

  • Professional coaching: structured guidance, skill development and practical learning plans.

Independent status

AIOU Coaching is an independent project. It is not Allama Iqbal Open University, does not represent AIOU, and is not endorsed by or affiliated with AIOU, a government department, a regulator, a court or a university. University names are used only to describe the subject matter of independent educational information.

Our approach to accuracy and integrity

  • We aim to link to official primary sources for changing dates, fees, policies and procedures.
  • We distinguish general guidance from official instructions and encourage readers to verify important requirements with the responsible institution.
  • Study support is intended to build understanding. Learners remain responsible for their own submissions and must follow their institution's academic-integrity rules.
  • We do not promise grades, admissions, employment, legal outcomes, search rankings or other results outside our control.
  • Public pages and client-facing materials require human review before approval.

Professional boundaries

General legal-information content, where provided, is educational only. AIOU Coaching does not claim to be a law firm and does not offer legal representation, court appearances or case-specific legal opinions. Matters requiring a qualified and authorized professional should be taken to that professional directly.
