Higher Secondary: FA, FSc aur I.Com

Intermediate level par subject selection aapke future degree aur career direction ko affect kar sakti hai. AIOU Autumn 2026 prospectus FA/I.Com aur FSc Pre-Medical/Pre-Engineering ko programme categories mein list karta hai. Current subject combinations aur eligibility prospectus se check karein.

FA

Humanities/social-science subjects mein concepts, definitions, examples aur structured arguments par focus karein.

FSc

Science subjects mein theory ke sath numerical problems, diagrams, practical concepts aur formula derivations practice karein.

I.Com

Accounting entries, business terminology, commerce concepts aur numerical working ko step-by-step samjhein.

Recommended weekly routine

  • Roz 2 focused study sessions, 35–45 minutes each.
  • Har subject ka weekly recap aur short quiz.
  • Numerical subjects ke liye daily written practice.
  • Language/theory subjects ke liye summary aur key terms.
