Matric: bunyaad mazboot karein

Matric level par focus basic literacy, numeracy, scientific observation, social understanding aur independent study habits par hota hai. AIOU prospectus mein Matric General Group aur Matric Science Group alag listed hain. Apne admission group ke mutabiq subject list aur course codes official prospectus se verify karein.

General Group

Har subject ke basic terms, definitions aur daily-life examples par focus karein. Urdu/English reading aur written expression ko daily practice dein.

Science Group

Science concepts ko diagrams, labelled parts, observations aur solved examples ke zariye samjhein. Mathematics mein har step likh kar practice karein.

Har unit parhne ka tareeqa

  1. Unit ka title aur objectives read karein.
  2. Unknown words ki Roman English glossary banayein.
  3. Textbook examples khud solve karein.
  4. Unit ke end questions bina dekhe attempt karein.
  5. Apni mistakes ki separate notebook banayein.
Matric ke course codes aur current book availability session ke sath change ho sakti hai. Exact code ko official Soft Books search mein enter karein.
